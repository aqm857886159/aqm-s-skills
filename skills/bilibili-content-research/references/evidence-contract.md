# Bilibili evidence contract

## Evidence layers

| Layer | Supports | Does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| Metadata | title, owner, duration, public counters, pages | causality or content quality |
| Subtitle | timed wording when a track exists | visual action, speaker identity, perfect transcription |
| Comment | an individual audience signal | representative audience distribution or factual truth |
| Visual/audio observation | what occurs at sampled timestamps | creator intent unless explicitly stated |
